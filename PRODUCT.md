# UniCommu
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
adaptive
## Users
Students, verified faculty/department pages, and administrators within a university.
## Product Purpose
A responsible university community board, with separate community and official announcement feeds. No private or group chat.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Existing TypeScript / React Native / Expo SDK 57 project. Prototype only, in-memory mock data; database selection remains open. University email verification and moderation require a future backend. Never expose email publicly. Official announcements require a faculty. No adult content or harassment. Start with feed and announcements, scaffold saved, notifications and profile.
## Brand Commitments
Thai interface; purple, white and gray inspired by the university portal reference supplied by the user. Mobile first, also usable on narrow web screens. Reference personal data must not be copied.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable Thai, scalable text, minimum 48-point action targets, safe areas.
